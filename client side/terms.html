Citizen Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Purpose of the Application

This application provides early public warnings, live reservoir status, and rainfall prediction to assist downstream residents during monsoon flood and dam release events.

2. Prototype Advisory & Safety Guidance

This system is an automated advisory channel. In the event of an official flood alert, citizens must follow direct instructions from local disaster response units, police, and district administration.

3. User Responsibilities

Users agree to provide accurate mobile contact information for emergency notification dispatch and not to transmit or manipulate false telemetry alert data.

4. Service Availability

While we strive for 24/7 telemetry uptime, network conditions and local carrier connectivity may affect SMS transmission speed. The web app maintains offline cache support.

5. Emergency Inquiries

For immediate rescue or emergency disaster management, call 112 or the National Disaster Response Force helpline at 1077.
