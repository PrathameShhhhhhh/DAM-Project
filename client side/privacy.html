Citizen Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for the Community Dam Alert application, we collect your name, mobile phone number, and coarse geographic location coordinates if you choose to enable localized weather detection.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your contact and location information is strictly used to deliver emergency SMS flood dispatches, broadcast acoustic siren alerts, and provide localized rainfall advisories in your selected language (English, Hindi, or Marathi).

3. Device Permissions & Audio

The application requests access to browser notifications and audio playback to trigger emergency sirens. You may toggle audio sirens and notifications on or off at any time within the app settings.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or monetize personal citizen records. Data is shared exclusively with certified emergency disaster authorities during flood evacuation protocols.

5. Contact & Support

For privacy inquiries or account assistance, reach out to the citizen helpdesk at citizen-support@dam-safety.org.
