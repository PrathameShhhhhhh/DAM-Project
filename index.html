Smart Dam Hydraulic Safety & Flood Early Warning System

An integrated infrastructure safety platform combining live sensor telemetry, machine learning risk classification, automated acoustic sirens, and citizen flood warning dispatches.

Verified Admins Only

Server Side & Admin Portal

Operational hydraulic telemetry console for certified dam engineers, disaster response officers, and municipal operators.

  • Full telemetry ingestion & rate-of-rise (dh/dt) analytics
  • Random Forest ML risk engine & T-critical calculations
  • Spillway gate actuators & automated GSM dispatch log
  • Full system access across all server and client modules
Admin Portal Sign In
Public Access

Client Side & Citizen App

Minimalist community flood warning application for downstream residents, farmers, and local citizens of all age groups.

  • Simple mobile number and OTP sign in / registration
  • Live reservoir water level gauge with universal safety badges
  • Location-based rainfall predictions & 24h precipitation risk
  • Multilingual support: English, Hindi (हिंदी), Marathi (मराठी)
