Telemetry Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. System Scope & Telemetry Collection

The Reservoir Hydraulic and Structural Telemetry Platform collects operational telemetry from authorized hydrological sensors, water level probes, rainfall gauges, and spillway actuators.

2. Data Retention & Purpose

All sensor readings are processed solely for real-time flood risk evaluation, time-to-critical calculations, and emergency SMS dispatch to disaster response authorities.
