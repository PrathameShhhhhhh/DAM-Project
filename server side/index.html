Decision Pipeline & Hydrodynamic Classifier Architecture

The risk classifier processes multi-sensor parameters (Water Level, Rainfall Intensity, and Rate of Rise dh/dt) to evaluate structural risk class, compute failure probability, and solve deterministic time-to-critical head limits.

PHASE 01
Sensor Ingestion
Captures water head h(t), rainfall R(t), and battery telemetry across sensor nodes.
PHASE 02
Surge Preprocessing
Calculates discrete rate of rise dh/dt = [h(t) - h(t-1)] / dt.
PHASE 03
Ensemble Classifier
Evaluates decision boundaries across validated decision tree nodes.
PHASE 04
Risk & T-Critical Output
Computes operational risk level and T-critical = (H_max - h) / (dh/dt).
